# 🛡️ Feminism-Focused Safety System

## Academic Foundation

This safety system is built on peer-reviewed academic research about women's experiences in online dating:

### Key Research Papers:

1. **Gillett (2023)** - "This is not a nice safe space": investigating women's safety work on Tinder
   - **Finding**: Women perform extensive "safety work" - invisible labor to protect themselves
   - **Application**: Secret Friend system, Auto Check-in, Safe Exit Planning

2. **Doria (2023)** - "A New Platform for Sexualized Violence": A feminist analysis of dating app-facilitated violence
   - **Finding**: Dating apps facilitate sexualized violence through various mechanisms
   - **Application**: SOS Button, Block/Report System, Photo Verification

3. **Amirkhani et al. (2024)** - "Beyond Dollars: Unveiling the Deeper Layers of Online Romance Scams" (ACM CSCW)
   - **Finding**: Introduction of "body scam" concept in Iranian context - sexual abuse over financial gain
   - **Application**: Sextortion Protection, Body Scam Awareness, Privacy Protection

4. **Thompson (2018)** - Missing discourse of consent on dating apps
   - **Finding**: Dating apps normalize disregard for consent
   - **Application**: Consent Education Module, Consent Tracking

5. **Bicharanlou & Siasirad (2017)** - Iranian women's online privacy literacy
   - **Finding**: Iranian women face unique privacy risks due to cultural and legal context
   - **Application**: Privacy Protection, Anonymous Mode, Screenshot Protection

6. **ACM (2024)** - "Society Encourages the Killing of Girls Like Me": Layers of Victimization in Online Dating Romance Scams in Iran
   - **Finding**: Pre-marital relationships in Iran create additional legal/social risks
   - **Application**: Enhanced Privacy, Anonymous Mode, Cultural Sensitivity

## 🎯 13 Safety Features

### 1. 🤝 Secret Friend / Trusted Contact System
**Based on**: Gillett (2023) - women need someone to know their location

**What it does**:
- Choose a trusted person (friend, family member) who receives notifications
- They know when you're on a date, where, and with whom
- They receive emergency alerts if you trigger SOS

**How it works**:
1. Enter friend's Telegram ID or @username
2. System automatically notifies them when you:
   - Start a date
   - Check in (or don't check in)
   - Trigger SOS

**Why it matters**: Women report feeling safer knowing someone is monitoring their status.

---

### 2. 👥 Mutual Safety Buddy System
**Based on**: Feminist solidarity and mutual protection

**What it does**:
- Pair with another woman on the platform
- Both activate safety features for each other
- Mutual alerts in dangerous situations

**How it works**:
1. Search for a safety buddy
2. Send connection request
3. Both activate safety features
4. Receive mutual alerts

**Why it matters**: Creates a network of women protecting each other.

---

### 3. ⏰ Automated Check-in During Dates
**Based on**: Gillett (2023) - "planning for a safe exit"

**What it does**:
- System checks in with you at regular intervals (default: 30 minutes)
- If you don't respond, it notifies your secret friend
- If you indicate danger, it triggers SOS

**How it works**:
1. Start check-in before/during date
2. Receive periodic check-in messages
3. Respond with "I'm safe" or "I need help"
4. System acts accordingly

**Why it matters**: Provides automatic safety monitoring without manual effort.

---

### 4. 🆘 Panic Button / SOS Alert
**Based on**: Doria (2023) - emergency response to violence

**What it does**:
- One-tap emergency alert
- Notifies all trusted contacts immediately
- Provides safety tips in the moment

**How it works**:
1. Press SOS button
2. System sends alerts to secret friend and safety buddy
3. Shows immediate safety tips
4. Option to call emergency services

**Why it matters**: Quick access to help in dangerous situations.

---

### 5. 🔐 Safe Word System
**Based on**: Gillett (2023) - discreet signaling

**What it does**:
- Choose a secret word that means "I'm in danger"
- Send it in normal chat to trigger SOS
- Only you and your secret friend know it

**How it works**:
1. Create a safe word (something memorable)
2. If in danger, send the word in any chat
3. System recognizes it and triggers SOS
4. Secret friend is notified

**Why it matters**: Allows discreet signaling without alerting the abuser.

---

### 6. 📸 Photo Verification
**Based on**: Amirkhani et al. (2024) - preventing "body scam"

**What it does**:
- Verify your identity with a selfie
- Require others to verify before matching
- Prevent catfishing and fake profiles

**How it works**:
1. Send selfie with specific pose
2. System verifies it matches your profile photos
3. Receive verification badge
4. Optionally require others to verify

**Why it matters**: Prevents deception and ensures person is real.

---

### 7. 🛡️ Privacy Protection (Iran-context aware)
**Based on**: Bicharanlou & Siasirad (2017) - Iranian women's privacy concerns

**What it does**:
- Hide profile from strangers
- Anonymous mode (no real name)
- Hide exact location
- Blur photos until match confirmed
- Screenshot protection
- Auto-delete chats

**How it works**:
1. Enable privacy features in settings
2. System restricts who can see your information
3. Protects against legal/social risks in Iran

**Why it matters**: In Iran, dating activities can have legal consequences. Privacy is essential.

---

### 8. 💬 Consent Education Module
**Based on**: Thompson (2018) - "missing discourse of consent"

**What it does**:
- Teaches 5 principles of consent
- Interactive quiz to ensure understanding
- Tracks completion

**How it works**:
1. Access consent education
2. Learn about:
   - Clear consent
   - Reversible consent
   - Informed consent
   - Enthusiastic consent
   - Specific consent
3. Take quiz to confirm understanding

**Why it matters**: Consent is the foundation of healthy relationships.

---

### 9. 🚫 Block & Report System
**Based on**: Doria (2023) - reporting mechanisms

**What it does**:
- Block users immediately
- Report harassment, catfishing, scams, threats
- Track report history
- Automatic safety score adjustment

**How it works**:
1. Go to user's profile
2. Select "Report"
3. Choose type (harassment, catfish, scam, threats, uncomfortable)
4. System reviews and takes action

**Why it matters**: Quick response to inappropriate behavior.

---

### 10. ⚠️ Sextortion Protection (Body Scam - Iran specific)
**Based on**: Amirkhani et al. (2024) - "body scam" concept

**What it does**:
- Educates about body scam signs
- Auto-flags inappropriate requests
- Provides protection strategies

**Warning signs**:
- Rushing intimacy
- Pressure to send private photos
- Threats to share photos
- Trying to isolate you from friends

**Protection**:
- Never send private photos
- Report immediately
- Keep documentation

**Why it matters**: Body scams are a new form of abuse in Iranian dating context.

---

### 11. 📍 Date Safety Planning
**Based on**: Gillett (2023) - "planning for a safe exit"

**What it does**:
- Create safe date plans
- Share with trusted contacts
- Suggest safe public places
- Emergency exit planning

**How it works**:
1. Before date, create plan:
   - Location
   - Person's info
   - Start/end time
   - Transportation
2. Share with secret friend
3. Have emergency exit plan ready

**Safety tips**:
- Public places only
- Tell your friend
- Your own transportation
- Keep phone charged
- Have exit plan

**Why it matters**: Planning reduces risk.

---

### 12. 🏃 Emergency Exit Strategy
**Based on**: Gillett (2023) - "planning for a safe exit"

**What it does**:
- Pre-plan exit strategies
- Quick exit options
- Safe transportation

**How it works**:
1. Before date, identify:
   - Multiple exit routes
   - Safe transportation options
   - Backup plans
2. During date, if uncomfortable:
   - Use pre-planned exit
   - Trigger SOS if needed
   - Go to public place

**Why it matters**: Having an exit plan reduces anxiety and increases safety.

---

### 13. 🏆 Safety Score System
**Based on**: Community-based safety

**What it does**:
- Tracks user safety behavior
- Decreases score with reports
- Increases with verification and positive behavior

**How it works**:
- Start with 100 points
- Reports decrease score
- Verification increases score
- Low scores may restrict features

**Why it matters**: Incentivizes safe behavior.

## 🌍 Cultural Sensitivity (Iran Context)

### Unique Challenges in Iran:

1. **Legal Risks**: Pre-marital relationships are prohibited
   - **Solution**: Anonymous mode, enhanced privacy

2. **Social Stigma**: Dating is taboo
   - **Solution**: Secret friend system, discreet SOS

3. **Family Pressure**: Family may disapprove
   - **Solution**: Anonymous mode, hide from strangers

4. **Digital Surveillance**: Government monitoring
   - **Solution**: Privacy protection, screenshot protection

5. **Body Scams**: New form of sexual abuse
   - **Solution**: Sextortion education, photo verification
